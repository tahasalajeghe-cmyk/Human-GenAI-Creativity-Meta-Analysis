# Research Literature Library

## Project
Human-GenAI-Creativity-Meta-Analysis

## Purpose
Central repository of scientific literature supporting
the research, meta-analysis, and evidence synthesis.

## Directory Structure

- PDFs/: Original research papers
- Extracted-Text/: Extracted paper contents
- Metadata/: Bibliographic information
- Research-Notes/: Detailed analysis of papers

## Research Guidelines

1. Preserve original paper identifiers.
2. Record DOI and publication year.
3. Never invent missing information.
4. Distinguish reported findings from interpretations.
5. Maintain traceability to original sources.
6. Cross-reference relevant studies.
